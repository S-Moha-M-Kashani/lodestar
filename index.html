        Save the whole board as lodestar.json. If your browser blocks the download (some embedded viewers do), copy the JSON below and paste it into a file instead.

        Import cards

        Choose a Lodestar JSON file. You'll then decide whether to add its cards to the board or substitute the whole board.

        An Asana project export works too — drop it in as it comes. Each task and subtask becomes a task card, keeping its notes, dates and a link back to Asana; completed ones land in Done.

        Writing the file yourself, or asking an AI to generate one? It must match this shape:

        
      
        Only "title" is required per card — everything else gets a default: cards land in the Inbox as questions, "num" (the permanent C-012 ledger number) is assigned automatically, timestamps are Unix milliseconds. "categories" is optional too — it carries custom life areas ("h" is an oklch hue, 0–359), and importing adopts any your board doesn't have yet.

        Board history
        
      
        Every change is logged here, newest first. Restore any earlier state — moving around never deletes history, so you can always come forward again.
        
      
        

      
        
        
        Deleted boards
        
        
        A deleted board is hidden, never emptied — its cards and its chats are still in the database. Restore one to bring it back whole, dropdown and all. Delete permanently erases it with everything on it.
        
        
        
      
        

      
        
        
        Deleted cards
        
        
        Deleted cards are kept in the database and stay recoverable here. Delete permanently is the only thing that truly erases one — nothing else, not even clearing this browser, can lose it.
